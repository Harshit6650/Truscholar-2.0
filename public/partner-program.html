S08 · WHY NOW · NEP (A)
Why now

Digital credentials just went from nice-to-have to government-mandated

India's education sector is mid-shift — from paper to digital, and increasingly to AI- and blockchain-verified records. NEP 2020 and the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) turned that shift from a trend into a compliance requirement, running on the National Academic Depository (NAD) through DigiLocker. Institutions aren't deciding whether to digitise anymore — only how fast, and with whom.

NEP 2020 → ABC

The National Education Policy 2020 introduced the Academic Bank of Credits, pushing every recognised institution toward digital credit and credential records.

Runs on NAD & DigiLocker

ABC operates on the National Academic Depository, integrated with DigiLocker — verified, portable student records are becoming the default rail, not an add-on.

Compliance deadlines are live

UGC has already set deadlines for institutions to digitise and upload credit data — this shift isn't optional, and it isn't years away.
